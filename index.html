Playback

Background audio is best-effort in a browser: Android may suspend speech generation when the screen is off. For longer playback, allow PageWhisper to remain active and disable battery restrictions where available.

Pronunciation

Teach the voice how to say names and made-up words, for example Longxuan as Long shwen. Rules can apply to every novel or just one. They change what is spoken, never the text on screen.

Backup & restore

Your library, covers, saved chapters, pronunciation rules and settings live only in this browser. Back them up now and then, and always before clearing the site's data.

Pronunciation




















Type the word the way it should sound, using ordinary spelling. Rules apply to sections that are not prepared yet, so press stop and play again to hear a change straight away. Longer written forms are tried first, and rules for this novel come before the ones for all novels.

Brightness








This dims the screen below your phone's own brightness, which is handy for reading in the dark. A web app cannot make the screen brighter than the phone's setting, so use the phone's brightness control for that. It applies only in fullscreen, and the preview is a little lighter while this panel is open.











Chapters
















The list is read from the novel's own page, including its extra pages. Sites that hide the list behind scripts or block fetching can't be read, so use Build by number there: it makes the list from the chapter link pattern. Chapter lists are kept on this device and are not part of a backup; they can always be fetched again.











Cover




No cover yet








PageWhisper looks for the cover on the novel's page. Sites that block fetching can't be checked, so for those pick an image from your device. Covers are shrunk and kept on this device, and they appear only in your Library list.












Sleep timer















The volume fades out over the last 30 seconds, then playback pauses where you stopped, so you can carry on from the same sentence.
